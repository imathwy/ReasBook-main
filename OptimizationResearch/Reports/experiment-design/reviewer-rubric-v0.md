# Mathematical Reviewer Rubric v0

Check independently:

1. assumptions and quantifiers are preserved;
2. domains, codomains, scalar fields, norms, and typeclasses match;
3. no conclusion became an assumption;
4. the statement is not vacuous;
5. the claimed equivalence, implication, or specialization direction is valid;
6. canonical mathlib/Core APIs are used without changing meaning;
7. placeholders or unapproved axioms are absent;
8. uncertainty is reported as `unknown`.

Findings use severity, category, claim, evidence, required action, and blocking
status. The Reviewer is read-only.
