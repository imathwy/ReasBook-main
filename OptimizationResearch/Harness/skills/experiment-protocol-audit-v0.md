# Experiment Protocol Audit Skill v0

## Inputs

Hypothesis, baseline, experimental configuration, benchmark split, budget,
metrics, and stopping rules.

## Outputs

Protocol status, blocking findings, confounders, leakage risks, and conclusion
limits.

## Checks

- The hypothesis is falsifiable.
- One core Harness variable changes per comparison.
- Budgets and human rubrics are comparable.
- Negative and invalid runs are retained.
